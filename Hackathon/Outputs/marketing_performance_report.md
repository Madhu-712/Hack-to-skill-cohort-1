## 3. Key Findings & Platform Analysis

### **Instagram: The Awareness Engine**
*   **Best For:** Visual storytelling, brand awareness, and high engagement.
*   **Top Performers:** "Sneak peek" Stories (9.1% engagement) and "Fudgy Brownie" close-ups (8.3% engagement).
*   **Sales Impact:** Instagram drives the highest "top-of-funnel" interest through Reels and high-quality imagery.

### **Facebook: The Conversion Leader**
*   **Best For:** Driving direct actions and website traffic.
*   **Performance:** Achieved the highest **CTR (44.4%)**, proving highly effective at converting followers into visitors for blog posts and event pages.
*   **Sales Impact:** Best for "bottom-of-funnel" conversions and time-sensitive promotions.

### **Twitter: The Engagement Pulse**
*   **Best For:** Real-time updates and customer interaction.
*   **Performance:** Lower average reach, but excellent for leveraging user-generated content (Retweets) and quick announcements.

---

## 4. Overall Recommendations
1.  **Double Down on Instagram Visuals:** Continue using high-quality "food porn" style images and "behind-the-scenes" Reels to maintain high engagement.
2.  **Optimize Facebook for Sales:** Use Facebook primarily for link-heavy posts, such as "Order Now" buttons or catering inquiries, to leverage its superior CTR.
3.  **Cross-Platform Strategy:** Use Instagram to build the "craving" and Facebook/Twitter to provide the "buying" link.
